Before the Business X-Ray, watch this overview — it shows how handing AI **one map of your business** turns it from a tool you babysit into an employee that runs the work. Below the video is exactly **why that map matters and how it works** — then two quick questions to continue.

## Why you give your AI a business map

A business map is one file plus a set of department folders — a model of how your whole business runs and how it makes money (Leads → Appointments → Presentations → Sales). It's the single thing that turns AI from a *tool* into an *employee*.

### Without a map

- Your AI doesn't know where anything lives or how you make money, so you hand-hold every task. **You stay the router** — every request runs through you.

### With a map (how it works)

- A request comes in → your AI reads the map, opens the right department, pulls what it needs, and does the work — the way an employee would. That's how it handles most of your requests **without you in the loop**.
